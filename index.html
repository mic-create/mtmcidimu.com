About Our Centre

A Tradition of Faith-Driven Compassion & Unmatched Quality

Situated within the serene premises of St. Francis Catholic Church in Idimu, Mother Teresa Medical Centre bridges world-class clinical expertise with warmth and empathy.

Inspired by the selfless legacy of Mother Teresa, our institution provides patient-centered medical solutions within a serene, highly secure, and ultra-modern facility. Every detail—from our healing environments to our surgical suites—is crafted to ensure ultimate patient wellness.
